Nothing enters on evidence that has expired or been invalidated by an earlier entry, and nothing enters on an undecided criterion — undecided means acceptance was not established, and entry presumes it was. A deferred verdict is not undecided. A candidate carrying a deferred verdict enters only while it remains reversible.

A reversal is a change: it carries its own criteria, evidence and plan, and is not a privileged instant operation.
